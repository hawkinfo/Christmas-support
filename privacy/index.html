Winter Letters · Privacy Policy

Winter Letters Privacy Policy Effective November 26, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are Winter Letters is provided by independent developer zhifei Tao. For privacy questions or support, contact zifeit@163.com.

2. Information on your device No account is required. The app does not automatically send personal information to a developer-operated server. Favorite letter numbers and reading history are stored in your device's preferences. The app includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs. It does not request camera, photo library, location, or contacts access. All stories and wishes are bundled with the app; they are not sent to an online AI service.

3. Apple purchases Purchases and restoration are handled by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. The app verifies Apple-signed transactions on your device to determine access to the complete collection. The developer does not receive your payment card details or payment credentials and does not operate a server to collect purchase receipts. Apple may process account, transaction, and fraud-prevention information under its own policies and provide sales and platform reports through App Store Connect. System diagnostics and usage reporting depend on your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

4. Sharing and contacting us If you use Share, the selected story and wish are passed to the app or service you choose in the system share sheet. That service handles them under its own terms. Opening an Apple website may transmit ordinary network information, such as an IP address, to Apple.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond, provide support, and meet applicable legal obligations. Email providers also process these messages. Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, or unnecessary personal information. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Storage and deletion You can clear favorites and reading history in Settings or delete the app. Apple's purchase history is managed by Apple; clearing local records does not cancel or delete a purchase. Device backups may include local app data depending on your backup settings. The developer cannot access those backups. Reading history and favorites may not be recoverable after deleting the app or losing a device.

6. Children and updates The app is intended for general readers and is not a service specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Purchases must be made by someone authorized to use the Apple Account and may be subject to Apple's family purchase approval controls. If our data practices change, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information.

7. Contact and requests For support correspondence held by the developer, you may request access, correction, or deletion at zifeit@163.com, subject to applicable law. Manage local reading records through the app and your device. For Apple Account, payment, or refund questions, use Apple's support channels.
