Winter Letters Help & Support

Reading your letters The first 3 letters are free. One purchase opens all 24 English stories and wishes for year-round reading. From December 1–24, the home screen features the letter for your local date. In other months, it starts with letter 1. The Christmas countdown uses the date on your device.

Purchased, but still locked? Check your connection, select Restore Purchases in Settings, and make sure the App Store is using the Apple Account you purchased with. If the purchase is pending, wait for Apple to approve it. You do not need to buy again. Restoring a purchase does not restore favorites or reading history lost from a previous device.

Price unavailable? Check your App Store connection and try again. The app disables purchasing when it cannot retrieve a valid product. The first 3 free letters remain readable.

Favorites and sharing Open a letter and tap Save letter to favorite it. Use Favorites on the home screen to filter the collection. Share opens the iOS share sheet so you can choose a person or service.

Clearing records Clear Favorites & Reading History in Settings removes records on this device without affecting your purchase. This cannot be undone in the app.

Refunds and billing Apple handles payments and refunds. Visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ for refund requests, or contact Apple for billing help.
